Intro

Welcome to the GIC-4 Quiz! This quiz will help you determine your GIC-4 code.

You will see a coordinate grid with the x-axis labeled "feminine <-> masculine" and the y-axis labeled "intensity".
Please select the point that best represents your ideal gender expression.

What does "intensity" mean?
It indicates how strongly or how fully that characteristic is expressed. i.e., the magnitude or development of the trait.

You may omit either or both axes if they don't apply to you.
Omitting the x-axis means you don't view your expression as a gender binary spectrum
Omitting the y-axis means intensity doesn't apply to you.
